Size-Budget Waivers

Measured over code lines against the budgets in §2; function lengths follow its counting rule, which leaves both brace lines out (2026-10-29). A file’s layer follows its place in the tree: files under a dialogs/ folder are dialogs, use* files and handlers/ are hooks and handlers, a page’s top-level file under pages/<area>/ is a page orchestrator, and the remaining UI files are components.

No file carries a waiver. The waiver for pages/inventory/dialogs/PriceChangeDialog/PriceChangeForm.tsx (164 code lines against the dialogs alarm of 160) is withdrawn: the file measures 134.

No file and no function exceeds its layer’s alarm. pages/analytics/sections/EmployeesActivityChart.tsx was at 155 against the components alarm of 150 until its series colours, time axis and formatters moved into the sibling hook useEmployeeChartFormatting.ts (2026-10-29): they decide what the chart shows and how it reads, a different level from the card that lays it out.

Three functions sit above their band and below their alarm: DateRangeFilter 122 and MovementsSection 112 (components, band 100), and useEditItemForm 88 (hooks, band 80). PriceChangeForm 113, EditItemForm 102, PriceTrendCard 82, usePriceChangeForm 79 and EmployeesActivityChart 64 are inside their bands. The band is guidance, the alarm is the gate.

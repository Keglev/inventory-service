Project overview

What it is

An inventory management system for small businesses: items, suppliers, stock movements with a full audit trail, and analytics. Java 21 and Spring Boot on the backend, React and TypeScript on the frontend, Oracle Autonomous Database. It runs in production at www.smartsupplypro.de with a read-only demo login, in English and German.

Where it comes from

The rules come from my working experience in manufacturing and logistics: stock never moves without a recorded reason, items are deactivated rather than deleted so the history stays complete for audit, and prices changed by the system are marked as such. Each rule is a decision I made and recorded with its alternatives in the architecture decision log.

How it was built

Three roles, kept apart. A chat model designs and reviews: alternatives are weighed and specifications written there. An editor-integrated agent implements those specifications and reports what it did and where it deviated. Every domain and architecture decision is mine; I review every diff and decide what is merged. Where a specification turns out to be wrong about the code, the agent stops rather than forcing the change.

Why that is verifiable

Every claim in a specification is checked against source before it is acted on, and reversed decisions stay in the log — including the removal of Kafka once verification showed no service boundary for it. CI runs the full suite on both tiers and publishes the coverage reports on this site. The history is public and changes now arrive as reviewed pull requests that state what was verified and what was left undone.

What you can see in two minutes

The live application (demo login, no registration). The decision log. The pull request record, especially the CI-safety and hosting-migration series.
